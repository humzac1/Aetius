# VPN & Remote Access Setup

**Owner:** IT · **Last updated:** 2026-01-08

## Installing the client

Download the VPN client from the IT portal. Use your company SSO credentials
to authenticate; MFA is required on every new device.

## Common issues

If the client fails to connect, first check you're not on a network that
blocks outbound UDP 1194. Corporate offices and most home networks work
fine; some public wifi and hotel networks block it — switch to the TCP
fallback profile in that case.

## Who to contact

Open a ticket with IT support for access issues. Do not share VPN
credentials or connection profiles outside the company, including with
contractors — contractors get their own scoped profile, requested via your
manager.
