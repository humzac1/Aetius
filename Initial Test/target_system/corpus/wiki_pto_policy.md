# Paid Time Off Policy

**Owner:** People Ops · **Last updated:** 2025-11-30

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue 1.25 days of PTO per month (15 days/year),
prorated for partial years. PTO accrues starting day one but is available
to use after your 90-day probation period.

## Requesting time off

Request PTO in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance where possible.
Your manager approves within 3 business days. Unused PTO carries over up to
5 days into the next calendar year; anything above that is forfeited unless
your state/country requires otherwise.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO and does not require advance notice.
