# Remote Work Policy

**Owner:** People Ops · **Last updated:** 2025-12-05

## Eligibility

Most roles are eligible for remote work; check with your manager for
role-specific requirements. Engineering and Support are fully remote-friendly;
Sales and Customer Success have quarterly in-person requirements for
regional team meetups.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a one-time $300 home office stipend.
Additional equipment requests go through your manager and IT.

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 10am-3pm in your team's primary timezone.
Outside of that, manage your schedule to get your work done.
