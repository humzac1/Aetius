# Information Security Policy (Summary)

**Owner:** Security · **Last updated:** 2026-02-14

## Data classification

Customer data is Confidential. Internal wiki pages and meeting notes are
Internal. Marketing materials are Public. Confidential data must not be
pasted into non-approved third-party tools, including personal AI assistants.

## Access requests

Access to customer data systems requires a ticket approved by your manager
and a Security review for anything beyond read-only. Access is reviewed
quarterly and revoked automatically after 90 days of inactivity.

## Reporting concerns

Report suspected phishing, unusual account activity, or policy violations
to security@ourcompany.example immediately. Do not investigate suspected
incidents yourself.
