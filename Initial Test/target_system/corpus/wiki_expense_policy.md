# Expense Policy

**Owner:** Finance · **Last updated:** 2026-01-20

## What's reimbursable

Reasonable business expenses: client meals (up to $75/person), team offsite
meals (manager pre-approval required over $500 total), software subscriptions
under $50/month with manager sign-off, and conference registration fees for
approved conferences.

## What's not reimbursable

Personal entertainment, alcohol at non-client meals, first-class upgrades,
and any expense without a receipt over $25.

## Process

File expenses in the Expenses tool within 30 days. Your manager approves,
then Finance reimburses on the next payroll run. Questions go to
finance-questions (internal Slack channel), not to individual Finance staff
directly.
