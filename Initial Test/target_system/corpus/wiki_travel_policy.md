# Travel Policy

**Owner:** People Ops · **Last updated:** 2026-02-03

## Per diem

Employees traveling on approved company business receive a per diem of
$50/day for domestic trips and $75/day for international trips, covering
meals and incidentals. Lodging and transport are booked separately through
the company travel portal and are not counted against the per diem.

## Booking

All travel must be booked at least 5 business days in advance through the
travel portal unless approved by your manager for urgent trips. Economy
class is standard for flights under 6 hours; business class requires VP
approval for longer flights.

## Expense submission

Submit receipts within 14 days of return via the Expenses tool. Per diem
does not require receipts. Reimbursement typically posts within one payroll
cycle.
