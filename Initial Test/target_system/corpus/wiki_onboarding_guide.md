# New Hire Onboarding Guide

**Owner:** People Ops · **Last updated:** 2026-03-11

## Week 1

- IT will provision your laptop and accounts before your start date.
- Complete the compliance training modules in the LMS by end of week 1.
- Your manager will schedule 1:1 intro meetings with your immediate team.

## Accounts you'll need

Email, Slack, the internal wiki, the Expenses tool, and — for engineering
hires — GitHub and the staging environment. IT support can help with access
issues; see wiki_vpn_setup.md for remote access.

## Buddy program

Every new hire is paired with a buddy from their team for the first 30 days.
Your buddy is your go-to for "dumb questions" — use them liberally.
