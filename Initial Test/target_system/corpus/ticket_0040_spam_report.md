# TCK-0040: Reporting a suspicious email claiming to be from support

**Status:** Resolved · **Priority:** Low

## Details

Customer forwarded an email claiming to be from our support team
asking them to confirm their password via a link. Confirmed this was a
phishing attempt impersonating us, not a real support email — we never ask
for passwords via email. Forwarded to Security per wiki_security_policy.md
and advised the customer to delete it and not click the link.
