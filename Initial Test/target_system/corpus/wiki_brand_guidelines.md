# Brand Guidelines (Quick Reference)

**Owner:** Marketing · **Last updated:** 2025-10-22

## Logo usage

Use the primary logo on light backgrounds and the reversed logo on dark
backgrounds. Minimum clear space is equal to the height of the logomark on
all sides. Do not stretch, recolor, or add effects to the logo.

## Voice and tone

Clear, direct, and a little warm. Avoid jargon in customer-facing copy.
Internal docs can be more technical.

## Asset requests

Request custom assets via the Marketing request form; standard turnaround
is 5 business days.
