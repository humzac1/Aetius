# Office Locations

**Owner:** Facilities · **Last updated:** 2025-09-18

## Locations

- **HQ — Austin, TX:** badge access 24/7, front desk staffed 8am-6pm.
- **NYC office:** shared floor, badge access 7am-9pm weekdays.
- **London office:** badge access 8am-8pm weekdays, closed weekends.

## Visitors

Register visitors at least 1 business day in advance through the Facilities
portal. Visitors must be escorted at all times in Austin and London; the
NYC shared floor has its own visitor policy managed by the building.
